02 / FIELD GUIDE
01 ⌖

Pick your patch.

Start with a featured target or enter any sky coordinates. Choose a field wide enough to follow the object over time.

02 ▷

Let time do the work.

Play the observations or step through them. Background stars help you spot motion. Split, subtract or stack the images for another perspective.

03 ✦

Question the change.

A changing point of light is a clue. Check wavelengths, masks and original data. A visual candidate is a starting point, never a confirmed discovery.
